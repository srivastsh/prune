Prune

Who you follow, sorted by when they last posted.

Prune reads your following list through your own X session in this browser. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Starting…
    1. Sign in to x.com in this browser if you aren't already.
    2. Scan. Prune loads everyone you follow and checks each account's latest post. 1,000 accounts usually takes a few minutes.
    3. Review. Accounts are grouped into 30, 60 and 90+ days quiet. Star anyone you want to keep.
    4. Unfollow. The queue runs slowly with random gaps and a daily cap, so it looks like a person, not a script.
